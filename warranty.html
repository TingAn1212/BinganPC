Before it reaches you

What every machine goes through

This is the part most sellers skip. It's also why the failure numbers look the way they do.

  1. 01

    Bench test

    Every part is checked on its own before it goes into a machine. We only fit cards that pass.

  2. 02

    FurMark

    GPU under sustained load, watching for artefacts, fan behaviour and temperature creep that only shows up after minutes.

  3. 03

    Cinebench

    CPU under all-core load, so a bad mount or a throttling cooler shows up before you're the one who finds it.

  4. 04

    Game test

    We install and run free titles to check real-world frame rates. Paid games will require you to provide an account in order to test the game.

A metal card with a test certificate

Every build ships with a laser-etched metal warranty card. Scan the QR code on it and you get that machine's test certificate — its own FurMark, Cinebench and game scores, the parts list, the build ID and the dates. The QR code opens the certificate for that machine.

If something goes wrong

How a claim works

  • Message us with the build ID — it's on the card, and it tells us what's inside without you opening anything. Your term starts on the delivery date printed there.
  • We diagnose it — free with a warranty. A standalone diagnostic is S$50. A home visit adds S$25. We tell you exactly what failed.
  • We replace the part — one replacement per part within the term, using the same model or better.
  • Delivered back and set up — the same way it arrived the first time.
