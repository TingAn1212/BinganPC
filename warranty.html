  • Six months or one year, counted from the day we hand it over

Before it reaches you

What every machine goes through

This is the part most sellers skip. It's also why the failure numbers look the way they do.

  1. 01

    Bench test

    Every part checked on its own before it goes into a machine — a card that artefacts or throttles doesn't get used.

  2. 02

    FurMark

    GPU under sustained load, watching for artefacts, fan behaviour and temperature creep that only shows up after minutes.

  3. 03

    Cinebench

    CPU under all-core load, so a bad mount or a throttling cooler shows up before you're the one who finds it.

  4. 04

    Game test

    We install and run free titles to check real-world frame rates. If you want a paid game tested, we can — but you'll need to hand us your account for the install.

A metal card, not a paper slip

Every build ships with a laser-etched metal warranty card. Scan the QR code on it and you get that machine's test certificate — its own FurMark, Cinebench and game scores, the parts list, the build ID and the dates. Nothing to lose, and nothing to forge.

If something goes wrong

How a claim works

  • Message us with the build ID — it's on the card, and it tells us what's inside without you opening anything. Your term starts on the delivery date printed there.
  • We diagnose it — free with a warranty, S$75 without. You get told what actually failed, not just "it's fixed".
  • We replace the part — one replacement per part within the term, using the same model or better.
  • Delivered back and set up — the same way it arrived the first time.
