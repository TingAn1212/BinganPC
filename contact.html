Before you message

What helps us quote fast

  • The games you actually play — a title list beats a spec list.
  • Your monitor — resolution and refresh rate decide more than anything else.
  • Your budget — a real number, so we can tell you what it does and doesn't buy.
  • Anything you already own — a case, a PSU, a drive we can reuse.
Payment & collection

How paying works

A deposit secures your build once the quote is agreed — it's what we use to buy the parts. The balance is due when you collect and the machine is running in front of you.

Nothing is ordered until you've seen the fixed price and said yes. If a part turns out to be unavailable or dearer than quoted, we come back to you before spending anything.

Timeline

About a week from parts in hand, depending on the case and how busy the bench is. We'll give you a date with the quote.

Collection

By arrangement, in Singapore. We send the address with your quote and hand the machine over running, not boxed.

After handover

If something isn't right, tell us. Repairs, upgrades and cleaning are all part of what the workshop does.
